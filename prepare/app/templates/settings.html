Appearance

Light, dark or automatic (matches the device) is set with the switch at the top of every page. Each phone and browser remembers its own choice.

Access

  • Plain view: http://homeassistant.local:8999 — works on anything on the home network.
  • Phone app, works offline: https://homeassistant.local:8998 — turn on Serve https as well in the app's Configuration, then follow Phone setup on each phone.
  • Data: /data/prepare.db inside the app, with automatic backups in /data/backups. Both are included in Home Assistant backups.
