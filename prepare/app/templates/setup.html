Phone setup

To keep plans, contacts and the pack on a phone for a power cut, the browser needs a secure (https) address. Prepare signs its own certificate, and each phone has to trust it once. (This is separate from the Meal Planner's certificate - a phone needs both if you use both.)

Download the certificate

  • Settings → Profile Downloaded → Install, enter your passcode, Install again.
  • The step people miss: Settings › General › About › Certificate Trust Settings → turn on "Prepare Local CA".

  • Tap Download the certificate and let it save.
  • Settings, search for CA certificate (usually Security › More security settings › Encryption & credentials › Install a certificate › CA certificate).
  • Install anyway, pick the downloaded prepare-ca.crt. A "network may be monitored" notice is expected and harmless.
